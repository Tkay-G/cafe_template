    Our story

    We opened The Corner Cup in 2019 with one espresso machine and a rule: everything we serve should be something we would happily eat ourselves.

    Our beans come from a roaster two towns over. Our bread and pastries are baked in the back before we unlock the door. The soup changes with the market.

    Bring a book, a laptop or a friend. The corner table by the window is yours for as long as you like.
